# Chilean life insurers: portfolios and cross currency swaps

Interactive dashboard: https://flakinton.github.io/chile-life_insurers/

Quarterly investment portfolios of Chilean life insurers (CMF BEST, Circular 1835, converted to USD at the Banco Central observed dollar of each quarter-end) and their cross currency swap hedges by counterparty (notes to the annual financial statements).

`index.html` is a standalone page with the data embedded; it also works offline.

Caveats: swaps cover the six largest insurers only (MetLife at Dec-2023, Consorcio, BICE, Confuturo, Penta and Principal at Dec-2025) and show contracts outstanding at the statement date, so matured swaps are not included. USD notionals are approximate.
